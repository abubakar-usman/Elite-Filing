Elite Filing Website: QA Feedback & Revision List
Prepared for the dev team, based on a review of the live preview at elite-filing.vercel.app
against the approved content document.
How to use this list: Priority 1 items should be resolved before any further visual polish.
Everything after that can run in parallel once the content is reconciled.

Priority 1: Content Accuracy
Go through the entire live site against the approved content doc, section by section,
and flag every place the two diverge.
Replace the hero headline "Architecting the Future of Global Enterprise" with the
approved headline. If the team genuinely prefers this new direction, bring it back to
the content doc for sign-off before it stays live, rather than letting it drift on its own.
Verify or remove the stats bar claims: "20+ Jurisdictions," "$2B+ Assets," "99.4%
Compliance," "500+ Enterprises." None of these are in the approved content, and they
need to be backed by real, verifiable figures before we publish them.
Fix the service category grid so it shows exactly nine categories: Company Formation
& Registration, Tax Registration & Compliance, Trademark & Intellectual Property,
Registered Agent & Virtual Address, Business Banking & Payment Gateways,
Accounting & Bookkeeping, Ecommerce Business Setup, Growth & Marketing AddOns, and Business Consultancy & Strategy.
Remove or fold in the extra cards showing up outside those nine, like Corporate
Advisory, Business Solutions, Regulatory & Compliance, Digital Transformation, and
Growth & Expansion Services. These look like consultancy sub-items that got
promoted to top-level categories by mistake.
Confirm testimonial content with real clients before publishing anything. Specific
dollar figures and named results tied to a real person need their sign-off first.
Get copy sign-off on any section that wasn't in the original content doc, including the
Enterprise Industries carousel, the "Start / Operate / Scale / Expand" process block,
and the FAQ block.

Priority 2: Header & Navigation
Move to a mega menu for the primary navigation. We have too many services and
countries to hide behind a simple dropdown, and a mega menu will actually let people
browse the catalog instead of guessing where something lives.
Redesign the header. As it stands, it doesn't feel professional or inviting, and this is
usually the first thing a visitor judges us on.
Fix the logo. It currently doesn't fit its container properly and appears to repeat inside
the menu, which reads as a bug rather than a design choice.

Priority 3: Brand & Visual Direction
Bring in real photography and imagery across the site. Right now there are essentially
no images being used, which makes the site feel unfinished and hurts the professional
impression we're going for.
Fix the color usage. Our primary color is blue and our secondary is orange, so it's not
clear why gold is showing up as a dominant color right now. Bring the palette back in
line: blue leading, orange used sparingly for action, and drop the gold.
Pull back on how much orange is used overall. It's currently carrying too much of the
page, buttons, icons, and accents all at once, which makes the brand feel less like a
corporate services firm and more like a consumer app.
Clarify or remove the "STARTUP / ENTERPRISE / GLOBAL / GROWING" pills above
the hero headline. As shown, they don't seem to do anything.
Rework the hero messaging so it reflects the full range of clients we actually serve,
from first-time founders to multinational structuring, not just the enterprise end. The
current tone risks setting the wrong expectation.
Replace the star ratings on testimonials with something more fitting for professional
services, like verified-client badges or client logos.
Make the footer feel more elegant and considered. Right now it reads as an
afterthought rather than a proper close to the page.

Priority 4: CTAs & Page Structure
Use CTAs more deliberately and more often throughout the site. Every page and every
major section should give the visitor an obvious next step, not just the homepage.
At the same time, build a clear hierarchy between them. "Book Consultation"
currently repeats five or more times with equal visual weight, so nothing feels like the
primary action. Reserve the strongest button style for the two or three highest-intent
moments, and style the rest more quietly.
Make sure every page uses a proper heading structure, with one clear page heading
and a logical hierarchy underneath it, rather than everything sitting at the same visual
weight.
Review overall page length and section order on the homepage. Every section should
earn its place before we add more.

Priority 5: Technical & Performance
Run a full Lighthouse audit. Multiple full-bleed sections in sequence are likely hurting
load time against our 95+ target, and this only gets harder once real images are added
back in.
Check text-over-image contrast in the hero and category cards against WCAG AA.
Confirm SEO fundamentals are in place: single H1 per page, meta titles and
descriptions, schema markup, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt.
Do a dedicated mobile pass, especially the service grid, mega menu, and testimonial
carousel, since these are the pieces most likely to break down on smaller screens.

Priority 6: Process
Going forward, any copy or structural changes made during the build should be
reflected back into the content doc so it stays the single source of truth. Nothing
should ship that hasn't been reconciled against it.
